Non-Canon Zone

⚔ Deathmatch Arena

The Deathmatch Arena sits outside the main canon of FaB. Heroes can appear here without consequence to their canonical stories.

The one confirmed exception is Kassai, who canonically competed, won gold, and used it to hire an army for her return to Volcor.
